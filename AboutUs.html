Welcome To RCSC Kolhapur

Established in 1961 by Dr. Karmveer Bhaurao Patil under the Rayat Shikshan Sanstha, RCSC Kolhapur has established itself as a respected institution dedicated to providing quality education.

The college holds a prestigious A++ Grade from NAAC with an exceptional CGPA of 3.78 and is affiliated with Shivaji University, Kolhapur.
